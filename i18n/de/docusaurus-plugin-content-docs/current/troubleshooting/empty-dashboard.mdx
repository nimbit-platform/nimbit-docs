Find out why a dashboard or one of its widgets shows nothing. How a dashboard chooses its thing and time is in [Dashboards, smart dashboards and time](../visualize/dashboards.mdx).

## Symptoms

- Every widget is blank, or shows a dash or **No data**.
- One widget is empty and the others are fine.
- The thing you expect is not in the thing picker.

## Check in this order

### 1. Time range and data source

Look at the time bar at the top of the dashboard.

- The range is too short for a device that reports rarely. Choose **Last 24 hours** or wider.
- **Time travel** is on and shows an earlier moment. Select **Live**.
- The update mode is **Manual**. Select the refresh button.
- A banner and a **MOCK** mark mean the data source is **Mock data** or **File**. Open the data source menu and choose **Device**. Files are not kept after a reload.
- The bar reads *N widgets keep their own*. Those widgets ignore the bar. Open the widget's settings and check its own range.

### 2. The thing the widget reads

On a Smart dashboard, a widget either follows the picked thing or is pinned to its own.

- Open the thing picker and choose a thing that has sent data.
- A thing that implements only some of the interfaces shows coverage such as **2 of 3 interfaces**. Widgets on the missing interface stay empty.
- A pinned widget shows a pin and a thing name in its header. If that thing is not the one you look at, the widget will not change when you pick another.

### 3. Does the thing have data in that range?

Open the thing (**Monitoring › Things**). **Live values** shows the latest reading of each measure, and **last signal** its time. For a longer window, select the measure on the **Schema** tab and **Open in Explore**. See [Explore telemetry over time](../watch/explore-telemetry.mdx).

If there is no reading at all, the dashboard is not the problem. Go to [A device shows no signal](./no-signal.mdx).

### 4. The measure behind the widget

Open the widget's settings and compare its measure with the thing type's **Schema** tab.

- The measure was removed or renamed in a newer published version of the thing type. Pick it again.
- The measure exists only in a draft. A measure the platform does not know receives no data until the draft is published. See [Drafts, publishing and versions](../model/drafts-and-versions.mdx).
- The widget reads a property. A property is a fixed fact, not a reading, so it has no history. Only a property with **Timeseries relevant** on is added to the telemetry of the thing. See [Measure and property types](../model/measure-types.mdx).
- The widget aggregates a measure over a range, and no reading falls inside it.

### 5. Permissions and groups

You see a thing only if a group you belong to contains it with **Read**, or its thing type, or you were given the thing, or you created it. A role without permission to read dashboards shows no dashboards at all.

- The picker lacks a thing you know exists: ask an administrator to check your group memberships. See [Users, groups and roles](../administer/access.mdx).
- A dashboard is missing from a thing's **Dashboard** tab: it is not assigned to the thing or its thing type, or it requires an interface the thing type does not implement. See [Build one dashboard for every thing of a kind](../visualize/smart-dashboard.mdx#assign-the-dashboard).
- Everyone but you sees data: compare your role and groups with theirs.

### 6. Widget settings

Select **Edit** and open the empty widget. The preview updates as you change settings, and **Help** at the top explains each one.

- A required field, such as the measure or thing, is not set.
- A filter, for example on **Event types**, **Thing types**, **Groups** or **Connection**, matches nothing. An empty filter means all.
- Mock data or a file is mapped to other measures than the widget uses.

Every widget and its settings: [Widget gallery](../visualize/widgets.mdx).

## Still stuck?

Write to info@nimbit.de with:

- Your tenant name, the dashboard's name and the link to it. The link carries the time range.
- The widget type and the thing it reads, by name.
- The time of the last reading you expect, with its time zone.
- A screenshot of the widget's settings and of the thing's **Live values**.
