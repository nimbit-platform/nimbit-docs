Welcome to the documentation for the **NimBit ML**, an end-to-end platform for building **Time Series Forecasting** and **Anomaly Detection** pipelines, perform **Live Forecasting**, and **Automated Scheduling**. 

This system is designed to provide robust forecasting capabilities and real-time insights, allowing you to make informed decisions based on historical data patterns and detect unusual events as they happen.

### Key Features

- **Data Preparation and Credentials Management**: The system offers a seamless approach to preparing time series data, ensuring it is clean, structured, and ready for analysis. It also incorporates robust credentials management, allowing secure access to various data sources and safeguarding sensitive information during the entire process.

- **Time Series Forecasting**: The system leverages advanced forecasting algorithms to predict future values based on historical data, ensuring high accuracy and performance across various domains.
  
- **Anomaly Detection**: Automatically identifies anomalies or irregularities in the time series data, helping to catch unexpected patterns or errors in real-time.

- **Live Forecasting**: Offers real-time predictions and updates as new data flows into the system, enabling continuous forecasting and monitoring.

- **Automated Scheduling**: Integrates powerful scheduling capabilities to automate the process of data collection, model training, and deployment, ensuring that forecasts and updates are performed without manual intervention.

### Use Cases

- **Operational Planning**: Improve decision-making with reliable forecasts, whether for sales projections, energy consumption, or production scheduling.
  
- **Experiments and Runs Comparison**: Using **MLflow**, the system allows for tracking and comparing various forecasting models and runs. You can experiment with different models and parameters, logging each run to analyze and compare their performance effectively. This is particularly useful for improving model accuracy and understanding model behavior over time.

- **Live Monitoring**: Continuously monitor your time series data and obtain live forecasts for real-time insights.

This documentation will guide you through the different aspects of the system, from setting up your data to performing advanced analysis. Explore each section to make the most out of your time series data.
