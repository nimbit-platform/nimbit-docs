We rebuilt the IoT Breeze docs from scratch. They are organized by what you want to get done, written against the current app, and now available in German too.

<!-- truncate -->

## Find the page for your job

The docs follow your data, from a device to a decision. Each section answers one question:

| Section | Question |
| --- | --- |
| [Connect devices](/docs/connect) | How do I get data in? |
| [Gateways and edge](/docs/gateways) | How do I bring in machines? |
| [Model your data](/docs/model) | How do I describe my devices? |
| [Visualize](/docs/visualize) | How do I see my data? |
| [Watch and respond](/docs/watch) | What needs me right now? |
| [Automate](/docs/automate) | How does it act for me? |
| [Knowledge and AI](/docs/knowledge) | What can I ask? |
| [Administer](/docs/administer) | Who can do what? |
| [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) | Something is wrong. |

Every page does one job. A tutorial teaches by doing, a how-to gets one task done, a concept explains how something works, and a reference lists every field. The label at the top of each page tells you which one you are reading.

New to IoT Breeze? The [quickstart](/docs/get-started/quickstart) takes you from a simulated device to a dashboard and an alert in about 15 minutes, with no hardware. If you know your role, [start from it](/docs/get-started): operator, solution builder, developer or administrator.

## Start a guided tour from the docs

IoT Breeze has guided tours that walk you through a screen step by step. Pages that have a matching tour now link to it: select the card and the tour starts in your IoT Breeze. All tours are listed on [Guided tours](/docs/get-started/guided-tours).

## Links that open your IoT Breeze

If your IoT Breeze runs on your own domain, open the arrow next to **Open IoT Breeze** at the top of the docs and enter your address once. Links from the docs into the app, and guided tours, then open there.

## In German

Every page is available in German. Switch the language at the top. The German pages use the labels the German IoT Breeze shows, so what you read is what you see on screen.

## Tell us what's missing

Every page ends with **Report a problem with this page**. It opens an email with the page already filled in. We read every one.
