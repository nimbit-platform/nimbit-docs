Ask Nimble answers questions about your plant in plain words: what is in a hall, which pumps are offline, how a machine ran last week, what the manual says. Every answer shows its sources.

<!-- truncate -->

## Ask

Press Ctrl+I (⌘+I on a Mac) on any page and type a question:

- "What is in Hall 2?"
- "Which things are offline?"
- "How did Pump 7 run over the last 7 days?"
- "Why is Pump 7 offline?"
- "What does the manual say about the seal interval?"

Nimble shows the steps it takes while it answers, and lists the records, readings and documents it used under the answer. On any record in the app, **Ask about this** asks about that record. See [Ask a question about your data](/docs/knowledge/ask).

## What Nimble knows

Nimble answers from the knowledge graph, your live readings and your documents. It can only find what the graph holds, so the answers get better as you add to it:

- **Places.** [Map your sites and buildings](/docs/knowledge/map-sites): sites, halls and lines, with your things placed in them.
- **Outside data.** [Bring in an outside data source](/docs/knowledge/outside-source), such as maintenance orders from an ERP, and link it to your things.
- **Documents.** Attach manuals and drawings to a place or a record.

Everyone sees only the records they may open, in Explore and in Nimble's answers alike.

## Turn it on

Knowledge and Ask Nimble are switched on per tenant: a tenant admin turns on **Ask Nimble and Knowledge** in **Workspace › Workspace settings › Features**. See [Knowledge and AI](/docs/knowledge) for what it needs and [How Ask Nimble answers](/docs/knowledge/ask-nimble-answers) for what it reads and where it stops.
