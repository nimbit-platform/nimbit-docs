Draw a circle, rectangle or polygon on the map. While the geofence is active, IoT Breeze records an event each time one of its things enters or leaves it.

## Before you start

- You need permission to write geofences to see the **Geofences** entry under **Settings › Configuration**.
- The things must report a position. A thing that sends latitude and longitude sets this by itself. For other things, use **Set location** on the thing page.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings › Configuration › Geofences**, or open **Monitoring › Map** and switch to **Geofences**. Both open the same screen.
2. Select **Create geofence**.
3. Enter a **Name**, for example `Hamburg depot`, and optionally a **Description**.
4. Pick a **Shape**: **Circle**, **Rectangle** or **Polygon**, then draw it on the map.
   - Circle: click the centre, then click to set the radius, or set the **Radius** with the slider or the number field. The radius runs from 1 m to 50,000 m.
   - Rectangle: click one corner, then the opposite corner.
   - Polygon: click to add points, then click the first point to close it.
5. Set **Status** to **Active**. Entry and exit events are only generated while the geofence is active.
6. Under **Applies to**, choose **Tags** or **Thing type**.
   - **Tags**: pick the tags. The geofence applies to things with those tags, across thing types.
   - **Thing type**: pick one thing type. To limit it to some things, choose them under **Things**.
7. Check the counts of things and thing types the geofence covers, then select **Save**.

To change a shape, delete it on the map and draw a new one. Drag a shape to move it. Use **⋯ › Edit** or **⋯ › Delete** on a list entry. Deleting can't be undone.

## Check that it worked

The list header shows your count, for example `3 active · 5 total`, and the geofence lists the number of things inside it right now. When a matching thing crosses the border, a **Geofence** event appears under **Monitoring › Events**.

## If it doesn't work

- **The geofence won't save**: the form marks what is missing. A name, a drawn shape, and at least one tag or a thing type are required.
- **No events appear**: the geofence is **Inactive**, or the things don't match **Applies to**, or they have no position yet.
- **You want a person to follow up**: events alone don't open incidents. Turn on the shipped **Geofence dwell** policy. See [Set up an incident policy](./incident-policy.mdx).
