This section answers one question: who can do what in your tenant, and how do you change it? It covers people and their access, the keys that let programs in, and the settings that apply to everyone.

You only see the **Administration** entries your role allows. A role that cannot manage users does not show **Users**. If an entry is missing, ask a tenant admin.

## Understand
- [Users, groups and roles](./access.mdx): how roles, groups and organizations combine to decide what a person sees and does.

## Do
- [Invite a colleague](./invite.mdx): send an invitation with a role and group access.
- [Give a team access to its things](./team-access.mdx): build a group, add people and things.
- [Create an API key](./api-key.mdx): let a script or integration call IoT Breeze.
- [Brand your tenant](./branding.mdx): logos, favicon and colors.
- [Store and share files](./files.mdx): upload, tag and share files.
- [Delete data](./delete-data.mdx): remove recorded readings, or a thing together with its data.
