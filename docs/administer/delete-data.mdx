Remove recorded readings, for example simulator test data before go-live, or take a retired device out together with its history. A deletion runs as a job and cannot be undone once it has removed rows.

## Before you start

- You need the **Data deletion** permission. Only **Admin** has it. Without it, the **Data** tab says **Deleting data needs permission**.
- To delete a thing, you also need to delete things. **Admin**, **Manager** and **Modeler** can.
- If simulations are running on the scope, stop them first. Readings they send after the deletion starts would stay.
- Backups are not touched, and data already forwarded to other systems is not deleted.

## Delete readings

1. Open **Administration › Tenant Settings › Data**.
2. Under **Which data**, choose **All data**, **Simulated data** or **Data with a tag**. For a tag, enter the **Tag** and its **Value**.
3. Under **Of which things**, choose **A thing type** or **Selected things**. **The whole tenant** is offered only for simulated data or a tag.
4. Set the **Time range**: **All time**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or a custom range.
5. Check **What will be deleted**, including the estimate.
6. Enter a **Reason** of at least 3 characters. It is kept in the audit log.
7. Under **Start**, choose **In 10 minutes (cancellable)** or **Immediately**.
8. Type the phrase shown under **Type … to confirm**, then select **Schedule deletion**.

Optional in step 3: tick **Also delete the things themselves**. The things go with every reading they recorded.

## Delete a thing with its data

1. Open the thing and select **Delete thing**.
2. Tick **Also delete all recorded data of this thing**. Without it, only the thing goes and its data stays in storage until its retention ends.
3. Enter a **Reason**, choose when to **Start**, and type the thing's ID to confirm.
4. Select **Delete thing and data**.

The option appears only if you have the **Data deletion** permission.

## Cancel or follow a deletion

Open **Administration › Tenant Settings › Schedules**. The job is listed there. With the delayed start you can cancel it for 10 minutes. An immediate deletion can be stopped, but rows already deleted stay deleted.

:::warning Deleting data cannot be undone
Check the scope and time range in **What will be deleted** before you confirm. Charts, exports, reports and rule history lose the data. Charts that read rollups switch to raw data for the affected time until the rollups have resynced.
:::

## Check that it worked

The job shows **Completed** with the number of rows deleted. Open a dashboard of the thing or thing type for the same range: the chart is empty.

## If it doesn't work

- **You cannot confirm**: simulations are still running on the scope, or the reason is shorter than 3 characters, or the typed phrase differs.
- **The job is Failed**: a data store could not be reached. Use **Retry from the last checkpoint** once it is back.
- **Disk usage did not drop**: space is reclaimed later by the retention sweep.
- **Nothing in this selection to delete**: no data matches the tag and range.
