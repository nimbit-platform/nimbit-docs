Make IoT Breeze look like your organization: logos, favicon, cover image and theme colors apply to everyone in the tenant.

## Before you start

- You need to update the tenant. Only the **Admin** role can.
- Have your images ready. The favicon is an `.ico` file of 48 × 48 pixels. Logos are PNG or SVG. The cover image is PNG, SVG, JPEG or WebP. Each image up to 5 MB, except the favicon.

## Upload images

1. Open **Administration › Tenant Settings** and stay on **General**.
2. In **Branding Images**, upload a **Favicon**, a **Default logo**, a **Light mode logo**, a **Dark mode logo** and a **Cover image**. All are optional.
3. Select **Save**.

The **Default logo** is used wherever a mode-specific logo is not set. Set the light and dark logos if your logo does not work on both backgrounds.

## Set the colors

1. Open the **Theme** tab.
2. Under **Tenant default appearance**, choose **Light** or **Dark**. People who chose their own appearance keep it.
3. Select a preview of a color mode in the theme editor and change its colors, layout and shadows. Edit light and dark separately.
4. Select **Save**.

Changes on **General**, **Features**, **Theme** and **Email** are saved together. A badge shows **Unsaved changes** until you save.

## Check that it worked

Reload the app. The browser tab shows your favicon. Switch between light and dark with the theme control in the header to see the colors of both modes.

## If it doesn't work

- **An image is rejected**: check the format and size above. The favicon must be exactly 48 × 48 pixels and an `.ico` file.
- **Colors did not change for one person**: they chose their own appearance in their settings.
- **You want the originals back**: **Reset to default** resets theme colors, branding images and email settings. It asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.
