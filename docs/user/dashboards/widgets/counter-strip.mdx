Up to six tenant figures in one bar: things, things online, messages today, events and what the tenant stores. Each counter opens the list it counts.

This widget is about the whole tenant. It is offered on Home and other tenant-wide dashboards, not on a dashboard about a thing.

## Settings

Add the widget with **Add widget**, in the group **Overview**. Select **Help** in the setup to read about its settings in the app.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Counters** | Pick up to six and drag them into order. **Reset to default** brings back Things, Things online, Messages today and Events. |
| **Time range** | Events, messages and average message rate cover the dashboard's range, or one of the widget's own. |
| **Card** | Background and border colours. |

Every widget also has a **Title** and, where it shows a card, **Appearance** settings for colours. Time-based widgets follow the dashboard's [time range](../time-range-and-filters.mdx) unless you set their own.

A problem shows in the line under the figure, for example *2 offline*. A dash with *No access* means your role may not read that figure.
