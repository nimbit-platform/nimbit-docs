You edit a canvas in place. Everyone else keeps seeing the saved version until you save.

:::info What you need
A role that may update canvases. The editor opens on a larger screen.
:::

## 1. Open the editor

1. Open the canvas from **Monitoring › Canvas**.
2. Select **Edit canvas**.

An empty canvas says **This canvas is empty**. Press <kbd>A</kbd> to add an element, or choose **Start from P&ID drawing** or **Browse templates**.

## 2. Add elements

The **Elements** library on the left holds everything you can place. Click an element to add it, or drag it to a spot. Use **Search elements and symbols** or press <kbd>A</kbd>.

| Section | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Data** | **Value**, **KPI**, **Gauge**, **Linear gauge**, **Fill level**, **Chart**, **Compact chart**, **Sparkline**, **State badge**, **State icon**, **State timeline**, **Thing card**, **Aggregate** |
| **Controls** | **Button**, **Switch**, **Setpoint**, **Hotspot** |
| **Draw & basic** | **Text**, **Note**, **Shape**, **Line**, **Zone**, **Image** |
| **Embeds** | **Dashboard widget**, **Canvas**, **Web page**, **VNC console** |
| **P&ID symbols** | **Tank**, pumps, valves and other process symbols |

## 3. Draw and connect

Draw shapes, zones and text, and connect equipment with pipes (press <kbd>C</kbd>). Snap and the grid line elements up. Hover a tool for its key.

## 4. Arrange

- Drag to move. Shift-click to select several elements.
- The bar above a selection aligns, distributes, groups, duplicates and locks.
- Right-click for **Bring to front**, **Bring forward**, **Send backward**, **Send to back**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Group**, **Lock** and **Delete**.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes.

**Layers** lists every element in drawing order. Rename, hide and lock elements there, and find those hidden behind others. Elements placed on an image nest under it and move and scale with it.

## 5. Preview and save

**Preview** shows the canvas as viewers will see it, in both themes. Commands in preview are dry runs: nothing is sent.

The status next to the buttons reads **Unsaved**, **Saving…** or **Saved**. Select **Save** to publish your changes to everyone who opens the canvas, or **Save and exit**.

If someone else saved the canvas meanwhile, the app says so. Reloading discards your unsaved changes. **Save as copy** keeps your work.

## Canvases managed by Nimbit

A canvas **Managed by Nimbit** shows **Update available** when Nimbit publishes a new version. Your edits stay; updates wait until you apply them with **Update to version …**. **Reset to Nimbit version** drops your changes. **Duplicate** gives you a copy that Nimbit no longer updates.
