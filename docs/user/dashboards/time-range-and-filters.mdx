## Pick the thing

On a Smart dashboard, the thing picker shows the thing the dashboard displays. Pick another thing and every widget that follows the selection switches to it. Pinned widgets stay. See [follow or pin](./smart-dashboards.mdx#follow-or-pin).

## Set the time range

Charts and other time-based widgets show the dashboard's time range.

1. Select the time range at the top, for example **Last 1 hour**.
2. Pick a preset, or set your own start and end.

Presets include **Last 5 minutes** up to **Last 5 years**, plus **Today**, **Yesterday** and other calendar ranges.

The range is part of the link. Share the link and others see what you see.

### Keep a range as the default

When you change the range and then save the dashboard in edit mode, a prompt asks **Save this time range too?**

- **Save with range** stores it as the dashboard's default.
- **Dashboard only** saves the layout and keeps the old default.

## Choose how it refreshes

Under **Updates** you pick the mode, and under **Refresh Rate** how often data reloads automatically.

| Mode | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Live mode** | Values arrive as they are reported. |
| **Interval mode** | The dashboard reloads at the **Refresh Rate** you choose. |
| **Manual** | Data reloads only when you select the refresh button. |

The bar reads like a sentence, for example *Showing … over the last 1 hour, refreshed live*. If you refresh again within a few seconds, the app asks you to wait.

## Widgets with their own range

A widget can keep its own time range. When some do, the bar reads *N widgets keep their own*.

Dashboard time is on for every dashboard, and the dashboard settings always ask for a timeframe. A widget follows the dashboard's range unless it has a range of its own. To make such a widget follow the dashboard again, remove the range from the widget's own settings.

## Look back in time

**Time travel** shows the whole dashboard as it was at an earlier moment.

1. Turn on **Time travel**.
2. Drag along the bar, or pick a date and time. **Previous reading** and **Next reading** step between values.
3. Select **Live** to return to now.

## Per-widget actions

Point at a widget to see actions in its top corner. You can download its data as CSV or open it full screen.
