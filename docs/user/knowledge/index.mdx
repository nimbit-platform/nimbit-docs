The knowledge graph is IoT Breeze's map of your business. It holds your **things** (devices), the **places** they are in (sites, plants, halls, lines), and other records such as assets, maintenance orders and documents. It also holds how they **relate**: this machine is in that hall, this order belongs to that asset, this manual covers that device.

Because the records are connected, you can ask in plain words: "Which things in Hall 2 are offline, and since when?" **Ask Nimble** answers from the graph, your live readings and your documents, and shows the sources.

## Turn it on

Knowledge is optional. Two switches must be on:

1. The installation has it enabled. Your platform administrator controls this. If it is off, **Administration › Tenant Settings › Features** says "Not available on this installation".
2. Your tenant has it on. A tenant admin opens **Administration › Tenant Settings › Features** and switches on **Ask Nimble and Knowledge**.

When both are on, **Knowledge** appears in the menu. When either is off, every `/knowledge` page sends you back to Home.

:::info Who sees what
- **Knowledge › Explore** needs permission to read Knowledge.
- **Knowledge › Ask Nimble** needs permission to read and to run Knowledge.
- **Settings › Knowledge setup** needs permission to edit Knowledge. Without it the menu entry is hidden; you can still open a setup page by its link and read it.
- Nimble and Explore only show records you are allowed to see.
:::

The built-in **Admin**, **Manager** and **Modeler** roles hold all of these permissions. **Viewer** can read and run Knowledge, so it can use Explore and Ask Nimble but not Knowledge setup. See [Roles](../administration/roles.mdx).

## Where things are

| You want to | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Browse sites, halls and things | **Knowledge › Explore** |
| Ask a question | **Knowledge › Ask Nimble**, or Ctrl/⌘+I on any page |
| Define what the graph holds | **Settings › Knowledge setup › Data model** |
| Bring in data from other systems | **Settings › Knowledge setup › Sources** |
| Check that data landed and is linked | **Settings › Knowledge setup › Data health** |
| Choose the AI model that answers | **Settings › Knowledge setup › AI models** |
