Ask Nimble answers questions from your knowledge graph, your live readings and your documents. It reads only what you are allowed to open, and every answer shows its sources.

## Open it

| Way | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Ctrl+I** (**⌘+I** on a Mac) | Opens Nimble in a panel beside the page you are on. Press it again to close. |
| **Ask Nimble** in the header | Same panel. |
| **Knowledge › Ask Nimble** | The full page, with your conversations on the left. |
| **Full page** in the panel | Moves the conversation to the full page. |
| **Ask about this** on a record | Opens Nimble about that record. |

Text fields keep Ctrl/⌘+I for themselves, for example for italic text, so the shortcut does not work while you type in one.

## Ask a question

1. Open Ask Nimble. An empty chat shows **Suggested questions** built from what your graph holds.
2. Type a question in **Ask about your graph…**, or pick a suggestion.
3. Watch the steps line, for example **Reading the hierarchy** or **Reading telemetry**. You can open it for detail.
4. Read the answer, then check its sources.

To focus on one record or place, use **Add context** to pick it. Nimble then shows **Asking about** that record, and its suggestions change to match.

### What you can ask

| About | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Records and places | "What is in Hall 2?" "Which pumps are there?" "Where is Pump 7 located?" |
| Counts and status | "How many things are there by status?" "Which things are offline?" |
| Live values and trends | "What are the latest values of Pump 7?" "How did Pump 7 run over the last 7 days?" |
| Causes | "Why is Pump 7 offline?" Nimble looks for what changed. It says when a finding is correlated, not proven. |
| Connections | "What is Pump 7 linked to?" |
| Documents | "Which documents cover Pump 7?" "What does the manual say about …?" |
| Maintenance | "Which maintenance orders are open in Hall 2?" |

Nimble answers from the records, readings and documents you can open. It does not know the rest of the world.

## Read an answer

The text names records you can open. Below it, depending on the question:

- **Rows**: the records it counted, with sort and paging. **View all in Explore** opens the full list. **Export CSV** saves them.
- **Charts and live values**: readings with a time range you can change.
- **Connections** and **Path**: how records link.
- **What moved**: signals that changed in a window.

Each record has actions: **Open record**, **Open in graph**, **Ask about this** and **Use as context** for your next question.

## Check the sources

Each `[n]` in the text points at a source under the answer.

| Source | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Graph** | A record or relation. |
| **Live value** | A reading, with the time it was read. |
| **Document** | A passage of a file. Open it to see the passage, a preview and **Open at page**. |

The footer states **Nimble reads only what you can open**. Open **How Nimble answered** for the steps, and **Details** for the model, the number of model calls and the run ID.

When Nimble could not back something up, it says so. Examples: **I didn't find any matching data.** **That's outside what I can answer from your graph.** **Not recorded**, when the field you filter on is empty. Answers also pass internal checks. A value that differs from its source, or a sentence with no source, holds an answer back, and the answer says why under **Held back**.

## Rate it and keep going

- **Helpful** or **Not helpful**, or **Right** or **Wrong**, tells the team that tunes Nimble how it did. For a wrong answer you can pick **Wrong data**, **Wrong approach**, **Incomplete** or **Unclear**, and add a note.
- **Ask next** suggests follow-ups. A follow-up keeps the context, so "and last week?" is enough.
- **Copy** copies the answer.
- **Conversations** lists earlier chats, grouped by **Today**, **Yesterday** and **Last 7 days**. Search them, or delete one. **New chat** starts clean; use it when you change the subject.

## Choose what Nimble uses

Above the question field, the **Reads** strip switches sources off for the next question. It sits in the same place in the side panel and on the full page. Hover a source to see its capability name:

| Button | Capability | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Records** | **Graph traversal** | Relations, flow tracing. |
| **Readings** | **Live values** | Timeseries, causes. |
| **Docs** | **Documents** | Manuals, datasheets, P&IDs. |

Looking up records is always on. A document scope picker (**Self**, **Inherited**, **Subtree**) sits beside **Docs**. It applies once you have added context and decides which documents Nimble reads around that record. The model menu in the question field's footer lists the **Agent** and **Model** choices; the default is **Auto**.

## Limits

- Counts, lists and lookups work without an AI model. Other questions need a model; if none works you see **This question needs an AI model**. A tenant admin can add one in [AI models](./ai-models.mdx).
- Long lists are sampled or capped. The answer says "N of M" and links to Explore for the rest.
- Documents that are not indexed cannot be read: wrong type, over 50 MB, or indexing switched off in Files.
- A question about something not in the graph gets no answer. Add the data: see [Sources and connectors](./sources.mdx).
- If your session expires while Nimble answers, you see **The answer did not arrive here**. Reload the conversation; the answer is saved.
- Answers are only as good as the links in your graph. See [Data health](./data-health.mdx).

:::tip Narrow it down
If a question fails or comes back too wide, name a place or a type: "Which `Pump` things in Hall 2 are offline?"
:::
