These tabs of **Administration › Tenant Settings** are about what runs in your tenant and what it is connected to. The other tabs are on [Tenant settings](./tenant-settings.mdx).

## Schedules

A read-only list of the task schedules of your tenant. Each row shows the **URI**, **Owner**, **Timing**, **Invocation**, **Status**, **Last run**, **Next run** and whether it is **Enabled**.

Timing reads, for example, `every 15m`, `cron: 0 6 * * *` or `once @ date`. If the tab says it could not load schedules, you may lack permission to view them.

## Data

Use this tab to delete recorded data. It shows the data stores to everyone who may read them. The delete form needs the **Data deletion** permission. Without it the tab says **Deleting data needs permission**.

**Delete recorded data** clears readings of the whole tenant, of a thing type or of selected things:

- all readings (**All data**),
- only simulated readings (**Simulated data**),
- or only readings with a tag (**Data with a tag**), within a time range.

The form is a short series of steps:

1. **Which data**: pick one of the three kinds above. For a tag, enter a **Tag** and its **Value**.
2. **Of which things**: pick **The whole tenant**, **A thing type** or **Selected things**. The whole tenant is only offered for simulated data or a tag, so clearing all readings needs a thing type or things. You can also tick **Also delete the things themselves**.
3. **Time range**: **All time**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or a custom range of days.
4. **What will be deleted** summarizes your choices. Check it, then confirm with **Schedule deletion**.

To delete one thing or thing type together with its data, use its delete action in **Settings › Onboarding** instead.

:::warning Deleting data cannot be undone
Check the scope and the time range before you confirm.
:::

## Packs

Packs are starter content from Nimbit: mail templates and blocks, interfaces, dashboards and canvases. Installed items belong to your tenant. Edit them freely.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not installed** | The item is not in your tenant. |
| **Current** | Installed and matches the shipped version. |
| **Update available** | Nimbit ships a newer version. |
| **Edited** | You changed it. |
| **Deleted** | You deleted it. |

Filter by **Content type** at the top. Then use the buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Install** or **Install missing items** | Adds the pack's content to your tenant. |
| **Reset** | Replaces an item with the version Nimbit ships, or creates it again if you deleted it. Your changes are lost. |
| **Uninstall** | Content stays as your own but no longer receives updates. Installing again creates new copies. |

You cannot uninstall a pack that other packs require, or whose content is in use. The dialog lists what blocks it.

## Connections

Provisioning and messaging providers configured for your tenant. Things use them to connect.

- The providers listed as **Managed by Nimbit** are internal. You can turn them on or off, but not delete them. Turning one off asks **Deactivate**: things using it lose their connections and can no longer send messages.
- Expand a provider to see how to connect. The **Tenant connection** shows an address with `<deviceId>`: replace it with the ID of the thing you want to connect.
- Credentials are not stored. Generated tokens are only available in the current browser session, so copy them when they appear.
